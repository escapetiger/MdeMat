\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
One-\/dimensional interval. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1geometry_1_1_interval}{Interval} represents a one-\/dimensional segment of the real line. It supports open, closed, and half-\/open intervals with configurable boundary inclusion properties.
