\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Third-\/order SSPRK integrator. 

\doxylink{classapprox_1_1odeint_1_1_ssprk3_integrator}{Ssprk3\+Integrator} implements the SSPRK3 method, a third-\/order explicit scheme for solving ordinary differential equations of the form\+:

\[\frac{\mathrm{d}(Mu)}{\mathrm{d}t} = Lu + F(u) + S(t),
\]

where $M$ is a mass operator, $L$ is a linear operator, $F(u)$ is a nonlinear operator, and $S(t)$ is a source term. This method is designed to preserve strong stability properties, making it particularly suitable for hyperbolic conservation laws and problems where monotonicity preservation is important.

SSPRK3 maintains the total variation diminishing (TVD) property under appropriate CFL conditions. The method uses three stages with specific coefficients designed to preserve stability properties of the spatial discretization.

avoiding spurious oscillations is critical.
