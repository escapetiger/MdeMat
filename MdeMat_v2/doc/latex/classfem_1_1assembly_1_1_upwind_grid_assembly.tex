\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Grid-\/based assembly for upwind schemes. 

\doxylink{classfem_1_1assembly_1_1_upwind_grid_assembly}{Upwind\+Grid\+Assembly} automatically selects left or right flux based on velocity direction for upwind discretizations. This class is specifically designed for convection-\/dominated problems where numerical stability requires upwind flux selection to prevent spurious oscillations.

The assembly chooses the flux direction based on the sign of the velocity component\+: positive velocities use left-\/biased fluxes (information flows from left to right), while negative velocities use right-\/biased fluxes (information flows from right to left).
