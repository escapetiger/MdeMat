\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_compiler}{Compiler} for spherical harmonic basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_spherical_harmonic_basis_compiler}{Spherical\+Harmonic\+Basis\+Compiler} specializes in compiling basis matrices for real-\/valued spherical harmonic basis functions. It creates and manages arrays of spherical harmonic basis functions including zonal and tesseral harmonics.
