\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Lebedev quadrature rule for spherical integration. 

\doxylink{classapprox_1_1integrate_1_1_lebedev_rule}{Lebedev\+Rule} provides highly symmetric integration points on the surface of a sphere in 3D space. This method is particularly efficient for integrating functions with spherical symmetry and offers superior accuracy compared to standard product rules.
