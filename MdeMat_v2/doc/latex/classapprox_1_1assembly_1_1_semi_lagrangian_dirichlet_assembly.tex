\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classapprox_1_1assembly_1_1_assembly}{Assembly} for semi-\/\+Lagrangian dynamic with Dirichlet boundary conditions. 

\doxylink{classapprox_1_1assembly_1_1_semi_lagrangian_dirichlet_assembly}{Semi\+Lagrangian\+Dirichlet\+Assembly} implements the assembly of transport operators for semi-\/\+Lagrangian methods with Dirichlet boundary conditions. It handles characteristic tracing, boundary intersections, and the incorporation of boundary data into the discrete system.

The class manages two key operations\+: volume assembly for interior characteristic tracing and boundary assembly for enforcing Dirichlet conditions. Characteristics that exit the computational domain are traced to boundary intersections where boundary data is evaluated and incorporated into the system.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Boundary condition assembly involves geometric intersection 

computations and requires careful handling of characteristic 

trajectories that exit the domain.
\end{DoxyNote}
