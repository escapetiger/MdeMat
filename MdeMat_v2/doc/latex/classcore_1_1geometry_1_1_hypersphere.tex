\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classcore_1_1geometry_1_1_geometry}{Geometry} for (d-\/1)-\/sphere domains. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1geometry_1_1_hypersphere}{Hypersphere} represents a generalized geometric surface that extends the concept of circles and spherical surfaces to arbitrary dimensions. It represents the set of points at a constant distance (radius) from a center point in d-\/dimensional Euclidean space. Dimensional representations\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item 0-\/sphere\+: A pair of points on a line
\item 1-\/sphere\+: A circle in a plane
\item 2-\/sphere\+: A sphere surface in 3D space
\item n-\/sphere\+: A surface in d-\/dimensional space
\end{DoxyItemize}
