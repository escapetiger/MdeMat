\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Fourier orthogonal basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_fourier_basis}{Fourier\+Basis} generates trigonometric Fourier basis functions up to a specified maximum degree. The basis functions include constant, sine, and cosine terms, orthogonal on the interval $[0, 2\pi]$.
