\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Chebyshev orthogonal basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1function_1_1_chebyshev_basis}{Chebyshev\+Basis} generates Chebyshev polynomial basis functions of the first kind up to a specified maximum degree. The basis functions are orthogonal on the interval $[-1, 1]$ with weight function $ 1/\sqrt{1-x^2} $.

The Chebyshev polynomials of the first kind satisfy the recurrence\+:

\[T_{n+1}(x) = 2x \cdot T_n(x) - T_{n-1}(x)
\]
