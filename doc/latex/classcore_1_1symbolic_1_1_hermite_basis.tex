\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Hermite orthogonal basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_hermite_basis}{Hermite\+Basis} generates probabilist\textquotesingle{}s Hermite polynomial basis functions up to a specified maximum degree. The basis functions are orthogonal on the interval (-\/∞, ∞) with weight function $ e^{-x^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi} $.

The probabilist\textquotesingle{}s Hermite polynomials satisfy the recurrence\+:

\[H_{n+1}(x) = x \cdot H_n(x) - n \cdot H_{n-1}(x)
\]
