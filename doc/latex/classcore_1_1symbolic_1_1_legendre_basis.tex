\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Legendre orthogonal basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_legendre_basis}{Legendre\+Basis} generates Legendre polynomial basis functions up to a specified maximum degree. The basis functions are orthogonal on the interval \mbox{[}-\/1, 1\mbox{]} with uniform weight function.

The Legendre polynomials satisfy the three-\/term recurrence relation\+:

\[P_{n+1}(x) = \frac{(2n+1)x \cdot P_n(x) - n \cdot
P_{n-1}(x)}{n+1}
\]
