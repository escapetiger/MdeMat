\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Shifted Maxwell-\/\+Boltzmann distribution function. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1function_1_1_maxwellian}{Maxwellian} represents a shifted Maxwell-\/\+Boltzmann distribution\+:

\[f(x;y,T) = (1/(2*pi*T)^{d/2}) exp(-|x-y|^2/(2*T))
\]

where $T$ is the temperature, $y$ is the center point, and $d$ is the dimension. This represents a Gaussian distribution centered at point $y$ with covariance matrix $TI$.
