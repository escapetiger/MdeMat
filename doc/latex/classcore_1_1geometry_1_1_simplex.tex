\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classcore_1_1geometry_1_1_geometry}{Geometry} for n-\/dimensional simplex domains. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1geometry_1_1_simplex}{Simplex} represents a generalized geometric domain that extends the concept of triangles and tetrahedra to arbitrary dimensions. It is the simplest possible convex polytope in any given dimensional space.

Dimensional Representations\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item In 1D\+: A line segment (2 vertices)
\item In 2D\+: A triangle (3 vertices)
\item In 3D\+: A tetrahedron (4 vertices)
\item In nD\+: An n-\/simplex (n+1 vertices)
\end{DoxyItemize}
