\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classcore_1_1function_1_1_function}{Function} representing the composition of two functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1function_1_1_composition}{Composition} represents the mathematical composition of two functions\+:

\[(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))
\]

where the output dimension of the inner function {\itshape rhs} must match the input dimension of the outer function {\itshape lhs}. The composition maps from the input space of {\itshape rhs} to the output space of {\itshape lhs}.

For derivatives, the chain rule is applied\+:   \[\frac{d}{dx}(f \circ g)(x) = f\textnormal{\textquotesingle}(g(x)) \cdot g\textnormal{\textquotesingle}(x)
\]

Higher-\/order derivatives use Faà di Bruno\textquotesingle{}s formula for the composition of functions.
