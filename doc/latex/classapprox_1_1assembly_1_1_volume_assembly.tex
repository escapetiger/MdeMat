\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classapprox_1_1assembly_1_1_assembly}{Assembly} for volume terms. 

\doxylink{classapprox_1_1assembly_1_1_volume_assembly}{Volume\+Assembly} handles the assembly of volume integral terms that arises in the finite element methods.

The class supports both grid and non-\/grid meshes, following the unified assembly procedure\+: iterate over elements, compute local volume integrals, insert them into the global sparse based on mesh connectivity.
