\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Spherical harmonic orthogonal basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1function_1_1_spherical_harmonic_basis}{Spherical\+Harmonic\+Basis} generates spherical harmonic basis functions up to a specified maximum degree. The basis functions are orthogonal on the unit sphere with respect to the standard spherical measure.
