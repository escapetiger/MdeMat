\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_compiler}{Compiler} for Fourier basis functions. 

\doxylink{classcore_1_1symbolic_1_1_fourier_basis_compiler}{Fourier\+Basis\+Compiler} specializes in compiling basis matrices for real-\/valued Fourier basis functions. It creates and manages arrays of Fourier basis functions including constant, cosine, and sine terms on specified intervals.
