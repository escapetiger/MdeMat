\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Scaled affine transformation of function space. 

\doxylink{classapprox_1_1space_1_1_scaled_affine_space}{Scaled\+Affine\+Space} extends the affine space with individual scaling parameters for both the coefficients and the directional component. Functions in this space have the form\+:

\[f(x) = \varepsilon_u \cdot u(x) + \varepsilon_g \cdot g(x)
\]

where $s_u$ are scaling factors applied to the finite element coefficients, $u(x)$ is the finite element function, $s_g$ is the scaling factor for the directional component, and $g$ is the directional shift.
