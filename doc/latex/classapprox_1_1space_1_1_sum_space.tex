\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Direct sum of spectral function spaces. 

\doxylink{classapprox_1_1space_1_1_sum_space}{Sum\+Space} represents the direct sum of spectral spaces of which each entry has the form\+:

\[f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^k \sum_{j=1}^{n_i} w_{i,j} c_{i,j}
\phi_{i,j}(x)
\]

where $\phi_{i,j}(x)$ are basis functions from space $i$, $c_{i,j}$ are coefficients, and $w_{i,j}$ are per-\/\+DOF weights.

The direct sum space  $W = V_1 \oplus V_2 \oplus \cdots \oplus
V_k$ has dimension $\dim(W) = \sum_i \dim(V_i)$.
