\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Grid-\/based assembler for semi-\/\+Lagrangian methods. 

\doxylink{classfem_1_1assembly_1_1_semi_lagrangian_uniform_grid_assembler}{Semi\+Lagrangian\+Uniform\+Grid\+Assembler} provides assembly methods for semi-\/\+Lagrangian finite element operators on uniform structured grids. This specialized assembler handles transport operators and boundary conditions for semi-\/\+Lagrangian discretizations.

The assembler supports both free transport operators and boundary condition assembly, with particular emphasis on handling characteristic tracing and interpolation required by semi-\/\+Lagrangian methods.
