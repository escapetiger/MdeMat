Detailed Description

Semi-Lagrangian scheme.

SemiLagrangianScheme implements a Semi-Lagrangian (SL) method for solving advection equations. The method combines the stability advantages of Lagrangian methods with the flexibility of spatial discretizations.

The SL method traces characteristics backward in time and uses high-order interpolation to evaluate solutions at departure points, making it particularly effective for transport-dominated problems with large time steps and minimal numerical diffusion.

Note
The semi-Lagrangian approach allows for larger time steps compared
to traditional Eulerian methods, making it computationally
efficient for advection-dominated flows.
