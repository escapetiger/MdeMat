1classdef UwdgScheme < physics.advection.AdvectionScheme
2 % UWDGSCHEME Upwind discontinuous Galerkin scheme.
3 %
4 % UwdgScheme implements an upwind discontinuous Galerkin scheme for
5 % solving advection equations. The method uses upwind numerical fluxes
6 % to handle the hyperbolic nature of advection problems, providing
7 % stability and sharp resolution of solution features.
8 %
9 % The upwind flux selection provides natural stabilization for
10 % hyperbolic problems but may require smaller time steps compared
11 % to semi-Lagrangian methods for stability.
12 %
13 % See also:
