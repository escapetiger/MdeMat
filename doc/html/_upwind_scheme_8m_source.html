2 % UPWINDSCHEME Upwind scheme.
3 %
4 % UpwindScheme implements an upwind scheme for solving advection
5 % equations. The method uses upwind numerical fluxes to handle the
6 % hyperbolic nature of advection problems, providing stability and
7 % sharp resolution of solution features.
8 %
9 % The upwind flux selection provides natural stabilization for
10 % hyperbolic problems but may require smaller time steps compared
