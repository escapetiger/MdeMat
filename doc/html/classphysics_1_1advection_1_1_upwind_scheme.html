Detailed Description

Upwind scheme.

UpwindScheme implements an upwind scheme for solving advection equations. The method uses upwind numerical fluxes to handle the hyperbolic nature of advection problems, providing stability and sharp resolution of solution features.

The upwind flux selection provides natural stabilization for hyperbolic problems but may require smaller time steps compared to semi-Lagrangian methods for stability.
