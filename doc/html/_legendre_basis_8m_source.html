2 % LEGENDREBASIS Legendre orthogonal basis functions.
3 %
4 % LegendreBasis generates Legendre polynomial basis functions up to
5 % a specified maximum degree. The basis functions are orthogonal on
6 % the interval [-1, 1] with uniform weight function.
7 %
8 % The Legendre polynomials satisfy the three-term recurrence

10 %
11 % \f[
12 % P_{n+1}(x) = \frac{(2n+1)x \cdot P_n(x) - n \cdot
13 % P_{n-1}(x)}{n+1}
14 % \f]
