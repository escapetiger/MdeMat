1classdef SldgScheme < physics.advection.AdvectionScheme
2 % SLDGSCHEME Semi-Lagrangian discontinuous Galerkin scheme.
3 %
4 % SldgScheme implements a Semi-Lagrangian discontinuous Galerkin (SLDG)
5 % method for solving advection equations. The method combines the stability
6 % advantages of Lagrangian methods with the flexibility of discontinuous
7 % Galerkin spatial discretizations.
8 %
9 % The SLDG method traces characteristics backward in time and uses
10 % high-order interpolation to evaluate solutions at departure points,
11 % making it particularly effective for transport-dominated problems
12 % with large time steps and minimal numerical diffusion.
13 %
14 % The semi-Lagrangian approach allows for larger time steps compared
15 % to traditional Eulerian methods, making it computationally
16 % efficient for advection-dominated flows.
17 %
18 % See Also:
19 % physics.OdeScheme, physics.advection.UwdgScheme
