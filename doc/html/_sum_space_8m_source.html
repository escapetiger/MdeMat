2 % SUMSPACE Direct sum of spectral function spaces.
3 %
4 % SumSpace represents the direct sum of spectral spaces of which each
5 % entry has the form:
6 %
7 % \f[
8 % f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^k \sum_{j=1}^{n_i} w_{i,j} c_{i,j}
9 % \phi_{i,j}(x)
10 % \f]
11 %
12 % where \f$\phi_{i,j}(x)\f$ are basis functions from space \f$i\f$,
13 % \f$c_{i,j}\f$ are coefficients, and \f$w_{i,j}\f$ are per-DOF
14 % weights.
15 %
16 % The direct sum space \f$W = V_1 \oplus V_2 \oplus \cdots \oplus
