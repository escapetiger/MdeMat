2 % HERMITEBASIS Hermite orthogonal basis functions.
3 %
4 % HermiteBasis generates probabilist's Hermite polynomial basis
5 % functions up to a specified maximum degree. The basis functions are
6 % orthogonal on the interval (-∞, ∞) with weight function
7 % \f$ e^{-x^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi} \f$.
8 %
9 % The probabilist's Hermite polynomials satisfy the recurrence:
10 %
11 % \f[
12 % H_{n+1}(x) = x \cdot H_n(x) - n \cdot H_{n-1}(x)
13 % \f]
14 %
15 % See also:
16 % core.function.OrthogonalPolynomialBasis,
17 % core.function.LegendreBasis
