Detailed Description

Geometry for n-ball domains.

Hyperball represents a generalized geometric domain that extends the concept of filled circles and solid spheres to arbitrary dimensions. It represents the region enclosed by an \((n-1)\)-dimensional sphere in n-dimensional Euclidean space. Dimensional representations:

  • 1-ball: A line segment
  • 2-ball: A disk (filled circle)
  • 3-ball: A solid sphere
  • n-ball: A solid region in n-dimensional space
