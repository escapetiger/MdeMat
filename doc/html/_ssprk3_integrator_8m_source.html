2 % SSPRK3INTEGRATOR Third-order SSPRK integrator.
3 %
4 % Ssprk3Integrator implements the SSPRK3 method, a third-order
5 % explicit scheme for solving ordinary differential equations of the
6 % form:
7 %
8 % \f[
9 % \frac{\mathrm{d}(Mu)}{\mathrm{d}t} = Lu + F(u) + S(t),
10 % \f]
11 %
12 % where \f$M\f$ is a mass operator, \f$L\f$ is a linear operator,
13 % \f$F(u)\f$ is a nonlinear operator, and \f$S(t)\f$ is a source
14 % term. This method is designed to preserve strong stability
15 % properties, making it particularly suitable for hyperbolic
16 % conservation laws and problems where monotonicity preservation is
17 % important.
18 %
19 % SSPRK3 maintains the total variation diminishing (TVD) property
20 % under appropriate CFL conditions. The method uses three stages with
21 % specific coefficients designed to preserve stability properties of
22 % the spatial discretization.
23 %
24 % SSPRK3 is particularly effective for problems involving
25 % discontinuous solutions or steep gradients where maintaining
26 % monotonicity and avoiding spurious oscillations is critical.
27 %
28 % See also:
29 % approx.odeint.ExrkIntegrator, approx.odeint.Exrk4Integrator,
30 % approx.odeint.HeunIntegrator
31
